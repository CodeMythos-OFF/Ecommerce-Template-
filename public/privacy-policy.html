1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopMaster ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our e-commerce platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you sign in to ShopMaster using Google or Microsoft authentication, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture
  • Authentication provider information

2.2 Shopping Information

When you use our Service, we may collect:

  • Shopping cart contents
  • Product preferences
  • Purchase history
  • Browsing behavior on the platform

2.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our Service:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address
  • Time zone settings

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
  • Process your transactions and manage your shopping cart
  • Send you order confirmations and updates
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests
  • Personalize your shopping experience
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage

Your information is stored using:

  • Session Storage: Authentication tokens and user session data are stored temporarily in your browser's session storage and are automatically deleted when you close your browser.
  • Local Storage: Shopping cart data and user preferences may be stored in your browser's local storage to enhance your experience.

5. Third-Party Authentication

We use Google and Microsoft authentication services to allow you to sign in to our platform. When you sign in using these services:

  • You are subject to the privacy policies of Google and Microsoft

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure authentication using industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocols

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:
